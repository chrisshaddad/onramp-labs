# INV-142: Invoice list

As a signed-in user, I want to see my organization's invoices, so I can follow up on what we billed.

## Acceptance criteria

- The list shows the invoices of the signed-in user's organization.
- Newest first, by issue date.
- 5 invoices per page, with "Next page" and "Previous page" links.
- A "Page X of Y" indicator.

Demo accounts: "Sign in as Acme" and "Sign in as Globex" on http://localhost:3100/login.
